LiveFX iPhone

A simple browser prototype for camera overlays and microphone tone effects.

Camera and microphone are stopped.

The photo is a manually positioned overlay. This demo does not perform automatic face tracking or realistic AI face swapping.

Voice effects

Choose a tone preset, then start the microphone. Use headphones to reduce feedback.

The audio effects are basic browser filters, not AI voice conversion or guaranteed male/female voice transformation.

Preset library

100 selectable filter presets. These adjust basic audio tone only.

Important iPhone limitation: This web page can use the camera and microphone only inside this page after you grant permission. A normal Safari website cannot replace the camera or microphone feed inside WhatsApp, FaceTime, Instagram, or other iPhone apps. For camera and microphone access, publish this page on an HTTPS website and open that address in Safari.
